About us

A run club
for people who
don't run.

Two Mile Run Club started in Atlanta, GA with one rule: keep it short enough that nobody has an excuse. Two miles is a distance almost anyone can finish on a Saturday morning — and once you finish one, you tend to come back.

Where it came from

The short
version

We kept hearing the same thing from friends: I'd come, but I'm too slow. Every club we knew of started at three miles and got faster from there, which quietly ruled out most of the people who wanted in the most.

So we cut the distance instead of the welcome. Two miles, one morning a week, same spot. The first run had a handful of people. Now the group is big enough that we split into paces and still finish together.

TMR reads two ways on purpose — Two Mile Run, and tomorrow. Run today, run TMR. It's a club, but it's really just a reason to come back next week.

What we hold to

Three rules

Rule one

Everyone finishes

No one runs alone unless they want to. A club runner stays with the back group every single week — that spot is a job, not an afterthought.

Rule two

Two miles is enough

We're not a training plan and we won't upsell you to a half marathon. Plenty of people here have never run further, and that's a complete way to be part of this.

Rule three

Talk to the new person

Showing up alone to a group of strangers is the hardest part of any run club. If someone looks new, that's your run partner for the day.

Who turns up

Probably
people like you

First-timers, ex-athletes coming back from a long break, parents with strollers, people who signed up for a 5K in a moment of confidence. Come on your own — most people did.

Questions

Before you come

How slow is too slow?

There isn't a cutoff. The walk-run group covers two miles in about 35 minutes and we wait for everyone at the finish.

Do I have to sign up first?

No — you can just turn up. Signing up gets you the meetup pin, a reminder the night before, and your bib number, so most people do it anyway.

Is it really free?

Yes. No dues and no membership. The only thing you might spend money on is coffee afterwards.

What if I don't know anyone?

That's the normal way to arrive. Find whoever is holding the sign and say it's your first run — they'll introduce you before we set off.

Can I bring my dog or my kid?

Leashed dogs and strollers are welcome with the walk-run group, where there's more room and a steadier pace.

What if it rains?

We run anyway. Lightning or ice is the exception — we post the call on Instagram by an hour before start time.

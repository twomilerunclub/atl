Two miles.
That's it.

Short enough to start. Often enough to stick.

Pace

Yours, not ours

Nine-minute miles or a walk-run all the way. The back of the pack gets a runner with them, every time.

Cost

Nothing, ever

No dues, no membership fee, no app to download. Turn up and you're in the club.

After

We stay a while

Coffee, stretching, arguing about shoes. The two miles are the excuse; this part is the point.

How it goes

Find us

Get there ten minutes early. Say hi to whoever is holding the sign — that's the whole check-in.

Pick your group

We split into three: running, jogging, and walk-run. Nobody checks your times and you can switch any week.

Two miles out and back

Flat, marked, and short. If you need to stop, stop — the route loops back so you're never stranded.

Stick around

We wait for the last person, take a photo, then head to coffee. Staying is optional but most people do.

Do I need to sign up to come?

No. You can turn up cold and run with us. Signing up just means you get the pin and the reminders.

Is there a fee?

None. There's nothing to pay now or later.

What if I'm slow?

The walk-run group takes about 35 minutes for the two miles, and someone stays with the back of the pack every week.
